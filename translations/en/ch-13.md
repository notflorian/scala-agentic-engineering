# 13. A minimal review packet to reduce back-and-forth

Before requesting a review from Codex, Claude or a colleague, always prepares a short package:

```text
Ticket/spec:
Goal:
Non-goals:
Changed files:
Base SHA / merge base / PR head SHA:
Base → candidate diff + untracked files:
Requirements → evidence (commands, results, reports, and SHA):
Tests run:
Tests skipped and reasons:
Residual risks:
Open questions:
```

Not only gives the diff. A review without spec produces generic comments; a review with objective, limits and tests more often produces actionable returns. First provide the contract and diff without the conclusions of the writer; the justifications come after the first independent analysis. The package must be regenerated when the SHA changes.

> **Why this package is not optional.** LinearB, *Software Engineering Benchmarks Report* (8,1 M+
> PRs, 4 800+ organisations), distinguishes assisted PRs, created by agents and unassisted PRs.
> (waiting before first care) is about 4,6× longer for assisted PRs; the PRs
> wait about 17,6 h vs. 3,4 h for non-assisted persons 5,2–5,3× according to rounding).
> may be larger ([408] lines against 157 at 75th percentile). LinearB acceptance metric
> is the ** percent of open mixed PRs within 30 days**: 32,7 % for IA PRs in category
> published against 84,4 % for unassisted persons. This is neither a success rate nor a rejection rate.
> definitive. Do not confuse categories: the gap of agentic pickup is not the figure of assisted PRs.
>
> Opsera, *AI Coding Impact Benchmark Report* (250 000+ developers, 60+ companies), studies the gains of
> RPs creation speed and the movement of the neck to the review. DORA notes that AI amplifies the
> existing foundations: flow gains do not imply an automatic improvement in stability.
> These reports remain observational, with different definitions and tasks; they do not demonstrate
> not causation for your deposit. Priority: short PRs, explicit contract, bounded WIP, proof of validation
> and reduction of time to production. The threshold 600 lines / 20 files is an alert ceiling,
> not a PR size target.

For the PRs, particularly monitor:

```text
- number of changed files
- diff size
- files outside the scope
- failing CI
- new tests that cannot fail
- unrequested refactor
- implicit change to a public or JSON contract
- build modification without approval.
```
